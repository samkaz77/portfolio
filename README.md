## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 📊 TweetLens Pakistan — AI Social Media Analytics (FYP)
An AI-powered social media analytics platform that collected and processed **13,000+ tweets from X (Twitter)** to perform sentiment analysis on three major issues in Pakistan: **theft, loadshedding, and water shortage**.

- **Tech:** Python, NLP, Transformers, Scikit-learn, Pandas, Streamlit
- **Features:** Sentiment & emotion analysis, city-based filtering, interactive dashboards, automated PDF reports
- **Highlight:** Final Year Project · Social Impact

### 🖼️ Pix2Pix — Sketch-to-Photo Translation
A Conditional GAN trained on **35,000 sketch-photo pairs** to convert hand-drawn sketches into photorealistic images.

- **Tech:** PyTorch, GANs, U-Net, PatchGAN, Computer Vision
- **Architecture:** U-Net generator + PatchGAN discriminator + L1 loss optimization
- **Highlight:** Deep Learning · Image Generation

### 🔬 Skin Lesion Classification — Medical AI
A multi-class classifier using **ConvNeXt-B4 and Vision Transformer (ViT-B/16)** to detect **7 types of skin lesions** from the HAM10000 dataset.

- **Tech:** PyTorch, ConvNeXt, ViT, Transfer Learning, Computer Vision
- **Techniques:** Transfer learning, data augmentation
- **Highlight:** Healthcare AI · 7-Class Classification
